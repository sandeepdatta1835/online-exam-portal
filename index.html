ABOUT THE PORTAL

A Simple Digital Examination System

The Online Exam Portal is designed to simplify examination administration for educational institutes, teachers and students.

Question papers can be managed centrally, while every student's answer sheet and result remains connected to their individual examination record.

  • Centralised examination management
  • Multiple students can receive one paper
  • Multiple answer-sheet pages supported
  • Individual student submissions
  • Marks and remarks management

📚 Examination Workflow

Admin → Student → Subject → Question Paper → Exam → Answer Sheet → Submission → Evaluation → Result


One question paper can be assigned to multiple students, while every student's answer sheet is stored separately.

Built for Organised Examinations

The portal keeps student accounts, examination assignments, submissions and results organised through separate administrative and student workflows.

🔐 Authentication Student and administrator login access is separated.
📂 Organised Records Examination submissions are connected to individual students.
📊 Result Management Marks and remarks can be recorded for completed examinations.
